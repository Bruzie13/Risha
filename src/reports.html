help Help guide

dashboard Dashboard

The state of the shop today: headline figures, today's sales and the items needing attention, the last 30 days of sales, products close to expiry, and open purchase orders. The Live chip turns red if the connection drops.

point_of_sale Point of Sale (POS)

Cash-only checkout, built for speed:

  • Left panel — search or browse products; click to add to cart. Out-of-stock and expired items can't be sold.
  • Right panel — adjust quantities, set a discount, enter cash received (use the Exact / +20 / +50 / +100 quick buttons) and the change is computed for you.
  • Scan barcodes anytime, or press F2 to focus the scan field.
  • Charge completes the sale, updates stock instantly, and shows a printable receipt.

inventory_2 Inventory

  • Filter with the status chips (Low Stock, Out of Stock, Expiring Soon, Expired, Healthy) and sort any column by clicking its header.
  • Reorder lets you pick low-stock products; after a final confirmation it creates the purchase orders and emails each one to its assigned supplier. Any that couldn't be sent (usually a supplier with no email address on file) are listed afterwards so you can chase them from the supplier's Performance panel. Expired items can be reordered — the new batch replaces the old one.
  • Use Import for bulk CSV product uploads, and the checkboxes for bulk reorder/adjust/delete.
  • Viewer accounts see inventory read-only; only admins and managers can add, edit, or delete.

receipt Sales history

  • Search and filter past sales by date; click View for item details and a printable receipt.
  • Void (admins/managers) cancels a sale with a required reason — the items go back into stock and the sale stays in history marked Voided, excluded from all totals.
  • End of day (also on the POS for cashiers) records the drawer count: the system shows the expected cash from today's sales, you enter what was actually counted, and any over/short is saved with notes for the daily record.

local_shipping Suppliers

Contacts, payment terms, and delivery performance per supplier. Click a supplier's email address or the Emails button to see every message sent to them with read receipts: ✓ delivered, ✓✓ the supplier actually opened or clicked it (automated mail scanners are filtered out).

Switch to the Orders view (beside the search box) for everything in motion with suppliers. Orders on the way shows each order with what the supplier has told you: the date they promised, any item they cannot fully supply, and their delivery receipt number. A late order is flagged. When goods arrive, choose Receive into stock. The same view lists offers a supplier has suggested, price changes waiting for your decision, and deliveries not yet paid. Suppliers with their own login confirm and ship orders themselves; for the rest, update the order here.

assessment Reports

  • Sales — pick a period (last 7 days, last 30 days, this month, all time, or your own dates) and view it by day, week or month. Shows revenue, number of sales, average per sale, items sold, best-selling products and sales by category. Export Excel saves the table as an Excel file.
  • Stock planning — forecasts what each product will sell over the next 30 days, using Random Forest and Gradient Boosting, which learn from the last four months of sales. What to reorder lists what to order, how much and by which date; Create purchase orders turns the list into orders and emails each supplier. Further down: products with too much stock, stock likely to expire before it sells, expected sales by category and brand, and how accurate the forecast has been.
  • Under All products, filter by how each product is selling and select any product to see its sales history and forecast. How these numbers are worked out at the bottom explains every figure.
  • Void requests (admins and managers) lists sales a cashier has asked to have voided, with the reason. Choose Void sale to cancel it and return the items to stock, or Keep sale to dismiss the request. Recently voided sales are listed below.
  • Activity log (admins) shows who did what, and when.

group Users & Roles

  • Administrator — everything in the back office, including users, the activity log, email settings and backups. Does not use the point of sale.
  • Manager — inventory, suppliers, reorders, voids; no user management and no point of sale.
  • Staff — the point of sale only. Signs in straight to the till; each sale reduces stock in inventory, low-stock items are marked on the product cards, and staff record the end-of-day cash count from the POS.
  • Viewer — can look at everything in the back office and change nothing.
  • Supplier — an outside login tied to one supplier. Sees only the purchase orders sent to that supplier and the products it supplies, and can confirm an order and mark it shipped.
  • Passwords need at least 6 characters with an uppercase letter, a number, and a special character. Five failed logins locks the account for a few minutes.

cloud_download Backups & Safety

A full database backup is emailed automatically once a day. Admins can also download one anytime from Settings → Preferences → Data Backup — do this before big changes. Every confirmed action (reorders, voids, deletes) asks before executing and shows a popup when done.

notifications Notifications

Alerts for low stock, stockouts, and expiring products. Open them from the bell in the sidebar or the Notifications page; suppliers with low-stock products are emailed automatically at most once per day.

keyboard Keyboard shortcuts

  • F2 — Focus barcode scanner input (POS page)
  • ⌘J / Ctrl J — Open Jump to…: search every page, every section of the page you are on, quick actions, and your products and suppliers
  • ⌘K — Same palette (search shortcut)
  • ↑ ↓ ↵ — Move through jump results and open one
  • Esc — Close any open modal or popup
  • Enter — Confirm the open popup
