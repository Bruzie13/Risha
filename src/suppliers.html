help Help Guide

dashboard Dashboard

The Dashboard gives you a real-time overview of your business — total sales, low-stock alerts, expiring products, and recent transactions. Use it as your daily command center.

point_of_sale Point of Sale (POS)

The POS page is a split-screen checkout system:

  • Left panel — Browse/search products by name or category. Click a product to add it to the cart.
  • Right panel — Shows the current cart. Adjust quantities with +/- buttons, remove items, or set a discount percentage.
  • Scan barcodes using a scanner or press F2 to focus the scan field.
  • Fill in customer name/phone (optional), select payment method, then click Pay to complete the sale.

inventory_2 Inventory

Manage your products: add, edit, view details, adjust stock, and set reorder levels. Use the CSV import tool to bulk-add products. The table shows SKU, name, category, price, stock, and expiration status.

receipt Sales History

View past transactions, search by sale number or staff name, and filter by date range. Click View on any sale to see item details and print a receipt.

local_shipping Suppliers

Track your suppliers, contact info, payment terms, and performance metrics (delivery time, on-time rate). View email history for purchase orders and alerts sent to each supplier.

assessment Reports & Analytics

Reports give you printable summaries. Analytics shows charts and trends — top-selling products, monthly revenue, and stock distribution.

notifications Notifications

Get alerts for low stock, out-of-stock items, and expiring products. Click the bell icon in the sidebar or visit the Notifications page to view and mark alerts as read.

keyboard Keyboard Shortcuts

  • F2 — Focus barcode scanner input (POS page)
  • ⌘K — Open global search
  • Esc — Close any open modal
